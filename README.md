# RemoteDesk

**Use your Mac from your iPhone.**

RemoteDesk is a native Apple remote desktop app. View your Mac’s desktop and hear its audio on your iPhone. When you connect a monitor, you can use the larger screen for the remote desktop or the built-in browser while the iPhone handles input.

## What you can do

- Pair your Mac and iPhone by confirming a matching code on both devices.
- Control your Mac with touch or a connected keyboard and mouse.
- Stream your Mac’s screen and audio over an authenticated, encrypted connection.
- Share clipboard content and transfer files between paired devices.
- Browse on your iPhone or on an attached display with the built-in WebKit browser.

RemoteDesk uses an iPhone app and a companion host on the Mac. Nearby connections work on the local network. Connecting from elsewhere depends on the network route and how the host is configured.

This repository is the public project overview. RemoteDesk is in development.
